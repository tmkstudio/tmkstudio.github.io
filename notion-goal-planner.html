Dashboard, goals and OKR-lite

The yearly goals dashboard puts the whole year on one page: every goal, its progress, its current milestone and the projects pushing it forward — open it and you know where the year stands. The goal database holds each goal with its reason, target date, milestones and a progress bar that fills as milestones land, turning a year-sized ambition into a staircase of finishable steps. The OKR-lite tracker gives each objective two to four measurable key results scored at the end of the cycle — the corporate system minus the corporate weight: no committees, no jargon, just ‘what does done look like, in numbers?’

Projects, 12-week cycles and reviews

The project tracker links every project to the goal it serves, so effort maps to outcomes — and a project that serves no goal shows up as exactly that, which is sometimes the most valuable information on the page. Monthly and quarterly review pages score the key results, review milestones hit and missed, and set the next cycle from what actually happened. Drift caught in March is a correction; drift caught in November is a eulogy.
