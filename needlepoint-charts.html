Small canvases are the friendliest way into needlepoint, and they are also the fastest route to a finished object you can actually give away. This set of needlepoint mini charts collects twelve cheerful counted designs — Strawberry, Lemon, Mini Toadstool, Smiling Star, Heart, Tulip, Goldfish, Anchor, Little Bee, Cherries, Moon & Star and Coffee Cup — each charted at roughly 30 × 30 stitches. At 18 mesh that is about 1.7 inches across; at 13 mesh about 2.3 inches, which is exactly the scale that suits ornaments, coasters, key fobs, magnets, pincushions and zipper pulls.

Every mini comes in two versions, a colour chart and a symbol chart, with a thread key giving DMC codes and the exact stitch count per colour. Treat the key as a starting point rather than a shopping list: minis are perfect stash-busters, and any wool or cotton you already own can be substituted freely. Finished sizes for both 13 and 18 mesh canvas are printed in the PDF, and a how-to page walks through tent stitch, blocking, and simple finishing ideas.

Why tent stitch minis work so well

All twelve charts are designed for tent (continental) stitch throughout, so there is one stitch to learn and the diagonal slant stays consistent across every design. Because the charts are counted and symmetrical in feel, they also double as small cross-stitch or plastic-canvas designs if you prefer a different ground. Beginners can finish a first mini in an evening or two; experienced stitchers use sets like this to keep a portable project in every bag.

Finishing ideas worth trying

Back a finished mini with felt and a loop of ribbon and it becomes a Christmas ornament. Mount two on cork discs and you have a pair of coasters. The Coffee Cup and Cherries make excellent pincushions when stuffed firmly with crushed walnut shells or polyester fill. Whichever finish you choose, block the piece first — a few hours pinned square under a damp cloth makes even beginner stitching look crisp and professional.
