Frequently asked questions

What format are the patterns in?
Most products are printable PDFs (US Letter). The pop-up card templates and the shadow box bundle also include SVG cut files, and the dollhouse printables include 300 DPI PNG sheets. Product pages list exactly what is inside each download.
How do I receive my pattern?
Instantly. Every product is sold through Gumroad — after checkout you can download the files right away, and a copy of the download link is emailed to you as well. No physical item is shipped.
How do I use a printable pattern?
Print at 100% (Actual Size) whenever scale matters — templates carry a 1-inch test square so you can verify your printer. Charts can also be followed on screen or in a pattern-tracking app. Each product includes its own how-to or guide pages.
Can I sell what I make?
The files themselves are for personal use and may not be redistributed or resold. Finished physical pieces may be sold at local craft fairs where the product page says so.
What if a file will not open?
Any standard PDF reader opens the patterns. If a download ever misbehaves, contact the studio through the Gumroad product page and the files will be sorted out.
