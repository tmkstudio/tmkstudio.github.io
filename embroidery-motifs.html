The hardest part of a small embroidery project is often finding a design that is the right size, the right style and clean enough to transfer without a fight. This hand embroidery motif pack solves that with 24 original line-art designs organised into four themes — Botanical, Woodland, Home & Kitchen, and Sky & Sea. You will find a leafy wreath, sunflower and lavender bunch alongside a fox face, owl, songbird and hedgehog; a teapot, watering can, cupcake and cottage; and a hot-air balloon, rainbow, whale, heart & key and crescent moon, among others.

Each motif prints at about 3.5 inches, the natural size for hoop art, pockets, tote bags, nursery textiles and quilt blocks — and because the designs are clean vector-style line art, they scale gracefully in your printer settings: 75% for a pocket or a cuff, 150% for a cushion panel. Every motif carries a suggested-stitch note telling you which areas suit satin stitch, where the French knots belong and which lines to work in stem stitch, so you are never left guessing how to translate a line into thread.

Transfer methods and stitch key included

The pack explains four reliable transfer methods — lightbox or sunny window, dressmaker's carbon, stick-and-stitch stabiliser, and iron-on transfer pencil — with honest notes on which fabric each suits best. It closes with an illustrated stitch key covering the six stitches used across the motifs: running stitch, backstitch, stem stitch, satin stitch, French knots and lazy daisies. If you are returning to embroidery after years away, that single page is a complete refresher.

Project ideas to start with

Stitch the songbird on the pocket of a plain shirt, the wreath around a name for a nursery hoop, or the whale on a child's tote bag. A row of three motifs — teapot, cupcake, cottage — makes a charming kitchen towel border. Because the motifs share one drawing style, mixing themes on a single piece looks intentional rather than random, which makes the pack a reliable well to draw from whenever a blank piece of fabric needs a personality.
