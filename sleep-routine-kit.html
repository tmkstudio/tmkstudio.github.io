Good sleep rarely starts at bedtime — it starts an hour or two earlier, in how the evening is shaped. This kit gives your evenings that shape without turning bedtime into a second job: everything is ready-made and timed. Pick the routine that fits tonight; the kit handles the rest, and a 30-night diary shows you what is actually changing.

Audit, basics and three wind-down routines

The kit opens with a 10-question sleep audit — a snapshot of your current evenings, so in two weeks you can compare instead of guess — and the sleep hygiene basics with real weight: anchoring your wake time, the caffeine tail, morning light, and protecting the bed's one job. Then come three wind-down routines: the full 60-minute version, the 30-minute weeknight workhorse, and a 10-minute emergency routine for nights that fall apart. Each is step-by-step, timed and free to run. A bedroom checklist covers the one-time fixes for dark, quiet and cool that pay you back every night.

Diary, detective work and the 7-day reset

The sleep diary and 30-night tracker take 30 seconds each morning; a detective page then shows you how to read your own data and change one thing at a time. A screens-and-light chapter offers a workable middle path — a curfew, not a ban — plus the morning anchor routine that decides when you will be sleepy tonight. The 7-day reset plan rescues schedules scrambled by holidays, deadlines or travel, and a final chapter covers the racing mind: the scheduled worry, the bedtime brain dump, and what to do at 3 a.m.

What this kit is not

This is a lifestyle organization tool — routines, checklists and trackers. It is not medical advice: it does not diagnose or treat insomnia or any other condition, and it is not a substitute for a doctor or sleep specialist. If you regularly struggle despite enough time in bed, snore heavily, or feel dangerously sleepy in the day, please get professional help — some sleep problems have medical causes no routine can fix. What the kit organises is the part that is yours: the evening, the room and the evidence.
