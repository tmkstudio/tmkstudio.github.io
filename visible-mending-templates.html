Visible Mending Templates — 24 Stick-and-Stitch Motifs + Boro Patch Sheets

A worn patch on a favourite pair of jeans is not damage to hide — it is a blank canvas in exactly the place you look every day. These visible mending templates give you 24 bold, simple motifs sized for repairs, each about 2 to 2.5 inches: heart, star, flower, toadstool, bee, rainbow, leaf, moon & stars, cat face, whale, cottage, feather, sun, mountains, fish, butterfly, acorn, tulip, rain cloud, fried egg, sprout, anchor, snail and cupcake. The shapes are deliberately chunky, so they cover holes and threadbare spots properly and read clearly even in a single thread colour.

The workflow is as simple as mending gets. Print the motifs onto water-soluble stick-and-stitch stabiliser (or trace them with a water-erasable pen), stick or pin the design over the damaged area, and stitch through stabiliser and fabric together. Rinse the finished mend and the stabiliser disappears — only your stitching remains. The pack also includes two boro patch sheets with printed running-stitch rows and a centre motif, for larger worn areas that need a proper patch rather than a single design.

What the how-to section covers

The included guide walks through the full repair: how to back a hole with a fabric patch before stitching, how to handle threadbare knees where the cloth is thin but not yet torn, which stitches hold up best (running stitch and seed stitch for texture, backstitch for outlines), and how to mend knits with stabiliser so the repair does not pucker. The templates work on denim, canvas, children's clothes and — with stabiliser — most knits.

Choosing a motif for a mend

Match the motif to the shape of the damage: a long tear suits the whale or the feather, a round worn spot disappears beautifully under the toadstool or the fried egg, and a cluster of small holes can become a whole scene — a sun, a cloud and a sprout turn a damaged knee into a tiny garden. Stitch in a contrasting thread for the full visible-mending effect, or tone-on-tone if you prefer your repairs cheerful but quiet.
