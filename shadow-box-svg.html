A layered shadow box turns flat cardstock into a little glowing world, and the magic depends entirely on clean files. This shadow box SVG bundle contains six complete scenes — Mountain Sunrise, Pine Forest Moon, Ocean Whale, Hot-Air Balloons, Winter Village and Night Owl — each built from six numbered layers sized at exactly 8 × 8 inches (20.3 cm). Layer 1 is the front frame and layer 6 the back, so assembly order is never a guessing game.

The files themselves are engineered for cutting machines: clean single compound paths with even-odd fill, compatible with Cricut Design Space, Silhouette Studio, Glowforge and other laser cutters. Every silhouette is connected to its own layer's frame, which means no loose islands drop out when you lift a freshly cut sheet off the mat. The back layers carry punched stars and moons that glow when you place an LED strip or a battery tealight behind the finished piece — the Night Owl and Pine Forest Moon scenes are designed around exactly that effect.

Assembly, briefly

Stack the layers from 6 up to 1 with 3–5 mm foam spacers hidden behind the frames, and display the stack in an 8 × 8 inch shadow-box frame at least 1.5 inches deep. The included 7-page PDF guide shows every scene's six layers alongside the assembled preview, suggests a colour order for each design, and lists cutting materials — cardstock in the 160–250 gsm range, foam board, or basswood for laser work — with spacer and assembly instructions.

Choosing colours that work

Depth reads through value, not hue: pick six shades that step gradually from light at the back to dark at the front (or the reverse for a silhouette-at-dusk look) and the scene will model itself. The Ocean Whale works beautifully in blues stepping down to navy; the Winter Village glows in pale greys and whites with one warm window colour saved for the back layer. Cut a full scene in plain white cardstock once, and you will understand the layering so well that colour versions become pure pleasure.
