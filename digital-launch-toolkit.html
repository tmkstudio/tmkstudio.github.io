Most first digital products are built in the wrong order: weeks of making, a price guessed in a minute, a listing written in a panic — then silence, which gets blamed on the product. This 22-page toolkit reverses the order: validate cheaply first, price with real numbers, write the page before you promote, then run one planned launch week instead of improvising.

Validate and price before you build

Part 1 gives you a six-question idea scorecard with build / test / park thresholds, three cheap tests (the post test, the sample test, the ugly page test), a five-question buyer interview script and kill criteria that save you from building the wrong thing. Part 2 does the pricing math with Gumroad's actual fees — direct sales versus Discover — including a net-per-sale table for prices from $5 to $49, how many sales reach a $100 payout at each price, and one fully worked pricing example end to end, so your price is a decision instead of a guess.

Listing copy and launch week

Part 3 covers the page itself: four title formulas with examples, a summary pattern, a six-part description skeleton, one fully filled example description annotated line by line, and an FAQ block that answers buyer doubts on the page. Part 4 is the launch: a day-by-day plan for seven days, six ready promo post templates (announcement, sample, story, objection, proof-of-work, honest close), channel notes on where to post them, and the after-launch review checklist that turns week one into information instead of emotion.

Who it is for — and what it refuses to promise

The toolkit is for you if you have a skill, a file or an idea and have never sold a digital product. It is not for experienced catalogue sellers, and it deliberately skips funnels, ads and automation tactics. It cannot tell you what will sell, and it contains no income promises and no revenue screenshots — the numbers inside are fee tables and arithmetic you can verify on Gumroad's own pricing page. If a table ever stops matching Gumroad's published fees, message the studio and it will be corrected or refunded.
