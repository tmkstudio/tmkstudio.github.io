Scale is everything in a dollhouse, and a wallpaper pattern that is even slightly off makes a whole room feel wrong. These dollhouse miniatures printables are drawn at true 1:12 scale (1 inch = 1 foot) and rendered at 300 DPI, so the patterns stay crisp and the proportions stay honest. The set contains five print-ready sheets covering the parts of a miniature room you reach for again and again: wallpaper, flooring, books, wall art and pantry boxes.

Sheet 1 gives you four wallpapers — Damask Diamond, Regency Stripe, Floral Sprig and Geometric Diamond — each panel 4 inches wide by 5 inches high. Sheet 2 covers floors with wood planks, checkerboard tile and parquet block. Sheet 3 is a small library: 18 book spines with titles plus 12 foldable book wraps that fold around foam board or balsa offcuts to make real, openable-looking books. Sheet 4 holds four framed pictures (landscape, seascape, portrait and abstract) and a 4 × 3 inch rug with a fringe edge. Sheet 5 stocks the pantry with six fold-up boxes — Sunny Oats, Pasta, Rice, Tea, Coffee and Sugar — plus a wall clock and a set of mini letters.

Print at 100% — the test square proves it

Every sheet carries a 1-inch test square. Print on US Letter at 100% (Actual Size), measure the square, and only then start cutting; the PDF places each sheet full-page so the scale is exact, and the individual 300 DPI PNG sheets are included for printers that handle images better than PDFs. The cover page carries full making instructions: gluing wallpaper and floors without bubbles, building the books and boxes, and mounting the art so it hangs flat.

Ideas for your first room

The Regency Stripe wallpaper with the parquet floor makes an instant parlour; add the landscape picture and the rug and the room is dressed. The checkerboard tile and the pantry boxes belong together in a kitchen. Because everything in the set shares one scale and one drawing hand, sheets mix convincingly across rooms — print the Floral Sprig twice and a hallway can carry the same paper as the bedroom, just like a real house.
